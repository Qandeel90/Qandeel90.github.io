Frequently asked questions

Is Sixtastic free?

Yes. Sixtastic is free to play. Optional video ads give rewards such as a second life or double level progress.

Can I play Sixtastic offline?

Yes. Matches against the computer, the World Cup mode and LAN multiplayer all work without an internet connection.

How do I hit a six?

Turn on LOFT, then swipe at the exact moment the ball reaches your batter. PERFECT timing sends the ball over the boundary.

Which cricket formats can I play?

Quick T20 style matches of 2, 5 or 10 overs, and a World Cup tournament with groups, semi-finals and a final.

Does it have multiplayer?

Yes. Two players can play 1 vs 1 on the same Wi-Fi network. No internet is needed.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 and newer phones and tablets.
