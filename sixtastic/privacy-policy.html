Privacy Policy

Sixtastic: T20 Cricket Game (package com.sixtastic.cricket) by Fun Game Portal.
Effective date: October 3, 2026

This policy explains what information the Sixtastic game ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information the game stores

  • Your game progress (level, matches, achievements, squads and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not run any servers and we do not sell personal data.

2. LAN multiplayer

LAN matches connect directly to another phone on the same Wi-Fi network. Match data such as scores and moves is sent only between the two phones on your local network and is not stored by us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional video ads that give in-game rewards. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location based on your IP address
  • Interactions with ads, and app performance and crash information

Google uses this information as described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone's Google settings, or at adssettings.google.com.
  • You can delete all game data by using Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the game.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Security

Ad data is sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK over encrypted connections. Game progress stays on your device.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
