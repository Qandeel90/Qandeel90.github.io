A funny chicken football game for Android

Flick, slide and score

Drag back from a hen like a slingshot and let go. Hens belly-flop across the pitch, knock rivals over and bump the ball into the net. Drag back onto the hen to cancel a shot.

Grow Mega Hens

Collect wheat and golden corn, then feed your hens at any time. Chick, Pullet, Hen, Mega Hen: bigger hens are heavier and hit harder.

2 players, offline

Play the CPU on Easy, Medium or Hard, pass and play with a friend on one phone, or link two phones on the same Wi-Fi with LAN Party.

Win the Cluck Cup

Four knockout rounds against tougher teams, with a big coin prize for the champions.

14 teams, 6 stadiums

Unlock funny chicken teams and play at Sunny Farm, Sunset Ranch, Frosty Coop, Moonlight Bowl, Beach Bash and Cactus Canyon.

Your match, your rules

Pick the match length (1 to 8 minutes), the turn time (10 to 30 seconds) and the goals to win, and spin the Daily Wheel for bonuses.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cluck Kick free?

Yes. Cluck Kick is free to play with ads. Optional videos give rewards like double coins, free coins, a cup second chance or extra daily spins.

Do I need internet to play?

No. Matches against the CPU, the Cluck Cup, 2 player mode and LAN Party all work without internet. Ads and reward videos need a connection.

How do I play 2 player soccer on one phone?

Tap 2 PLAYERS, pick a team each and take turns flicking your hens.

How do I make a Mega Hen?

Touch wheat on the pitch with a hen, tap FEED and then tap a hen. Each feed grows it one stage until it becomes a Mega Hen.

How do I play with a friend on another phone?

Connect both phones to the same Wi-Fi. One player taps LAN PARTY and HOST, the other taps JOIN.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 and newer phones and tablets.
