Shire Rider privacy policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how Shire Rider: Horse Riding (package name com.fungameportal.shirerider), an Android game made by Fun Game Portal, handles your information.

In short: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no analytics and no internet connection.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels finished, coins, stable upgrades, horse health, best Free Ride distance and your settings) is saved in a small file on your own device. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services inside the game.
  • No sign-in, accounts, chat or user-generated content.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • The game does not use the internet. The only Android permission it asks for is vibration, for the rumble you feel when you land or crash.

Google Play

You buy and download the game through Google Play. Google handles the payment and may collect information about the purchase under its own privacy policy. Fun Game Portal receives only the anonymous sales and install totals that Google Play shows to every developer.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
